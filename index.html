Pain changes how you move. We retrain the movement.

When something hurts, your brain quietly hands the work to other muscles and joints. It helps for a few days, then becomes the next problem. We find where control was lost and rebuild it.

What we treat, and how

Manual therapy

Skilled hands-on techniques to ease pain, free stiff joints and settle tight muscles, so you can move without guarding.

Kinetic Control

A movement assessment that shows where your body loses control, then targeted retraining until the right muscles do the right job.

Movement and sports rehab

A structured return to training after strains, sprains and overuse injuries.

After injury or surgery

Step-by-step recovery of strength, range of motion and confidence.

Back, neck and posture

Desk-day pain, stiffness and posture habits, treated at the source.

How your care unfolds

  1. 1

    Assess

    We listen, then watch you move. Your history and a movement screen show what is really driving the pain.

  2. 2

    Retrain

    Manual therapy settles the area. Control exercises teach it to work properly again.

  3. 3

    Return

    You progress back to work, sport and daily life with a plan that keeps the problem from coming back.

Before your first visit

What happens in the first session?

We talk through your pain and history, assess how you move, and agree a plan with you.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like shorts and a t-shirt, so we can see the area we are assessing.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what the assessment finds. We tell you honestly after your first visit.
